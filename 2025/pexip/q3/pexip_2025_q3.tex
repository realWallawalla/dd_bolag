\section{Company Overview}
CellaVision offers scalable solutions that redefine and transform the process of performing blood differentials for labs of all sizes.
Products:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Analyzers: analyzing blood samples.
	\item Software: Image based software for blood smears. Works with own analyzers or other oems.
	\item Reagents: Reagents for analysing blood samples. Works with own analyzers or other oems.
\end{itemize}
